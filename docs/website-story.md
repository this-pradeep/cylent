# Cylent Solutions — Website Story

## Purpose

This document defines the narrative structure of the website.

The website should not feel like a collection of sections.

It should feel like a journey.

Visitors should leave understanding:

- Who we are
- What we do
- How we think
- Why our work is different
- Why they should trust us

The story should be experienced, not explained.

---

# The Core Narrative

Most agencies offer services.

We create experiences.

Technology, visuals, and storytelling are often treated as separate disciplines.

We believe they are strongest when they work together.

Every project is the result of three forces:

- Strategy
- Creativity
- Execution

This philosophy should be reflected throughout the website.

---

# Story Structure

## Chapter 1 — First Impression

### Objective

Capture attention immediately.

### User Thought

"This doesn't feel like a typical agency website."

Minimal distractions.

Clear message.

Confident presentation.

- Buzzword-heavy headlines

## Chapter 6 — Proof

### Objective

Demonstrate capability.

Show real work.

Avoid:

- Endless project grids
- Generic case study cards

Present work in a way that feels curated.

Quality over quantity.

### Structure

One chapter per discipline, in the order the navbar lists them — Web, Video, Design. Each
chapter opens on its pillar verb and claim from Chapter 4, then proves it:

- **One lead project per discipline** takes a full frame, and carries all four beats
  (challenge, process, solution, result) arriving as it is scrolled through.
- **Everything else is supporting**, shown compactly with a single line.

A discipline is claimed and evidenced in one place, never twice. The lead-and-supporting
split is the portfolio philosophy made structural: a few exceptional projects are worth more
than many average ones, so exactly one project per discipline gets the screen.

### User Thought

"Their work speaks for itself."

# Experience Guidelines

The website should feel:

- Premium
- Modern
- Intentional
- Creative
- Human

Not:

- Corporate
- Overly technical
- Overly artistic
- Overwhelming

Balance creativity with clarity.

---

# Narrative Rule

Every section must answer one question:

Why should the visitor continue scrolling?

If a section does not move the story forward, redesign or remove it.

---

# Final Takeaway

When visitors leave the website, they should not remember a list of services.

They should remember a feeling.

A feeling that Cylent Solutions creates work with care, creativity, and craftsmanship.

The website itself should be the first proof of that promise.
