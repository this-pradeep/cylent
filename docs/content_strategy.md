# CONTENT STRATEGY

## Core Voice

Precise.

Confident.

Human.

---

# Headlines

Prefer short statements.

Examples:

"Complex problems.
Better engineered."

"Software built for what comes next."

"Architecture before implementation."

"Built to scale.
Designed to last."

"Have something difficult to build?"

"Let's engineer it."

---

# Avoid

"We are a leading IT company..."

"We provide end-to-end solutions..."

"Our highly skilled team..."

"We leverage cutting-edge technologies..."

These phrases are generic.

---

# Service Copy

Describe outcomes and engineering decisions.

Not just technologies.

Bad:

"We use React and Node.js."

Better:

"We design production-grade applications around maintainable architecture, predictable performance and long-term scale."

---

# Project Copy

Every project should answer:

What was the problem?

Why was it difficult?

What did Cylent engineer?

What technology was required?

What changed?

---

# Technical Language

Technical details are encouraged.

But always explain why they matter.

---

# Trust

Never fabricate:

clients
statistics
awards
revenue
users
performance numbers

Only use verified information.
